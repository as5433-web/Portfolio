My approach

Connecting theory
with practical work

I like following an idea through the full process: understanding the circuit, building it, checking its behavior, and working through problems.

Analog audio systems gives that process a particular direction. It connects my interest in electronics with sound and music, and it is an area I want to keep exploring as my education progresses.

My background also includes assembling computers, restoring electronic devices, and diagnosing mechanical problems. Those experiences complement the structured design and measurement work I have done in class.
